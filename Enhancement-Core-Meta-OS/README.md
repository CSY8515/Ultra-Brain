# Enhancement Core Meta OS

## Future scope

Enhancement Core Meta OS is planned for v0.3 to provide governed capability
augmentation after the Safety baseline is established. Its future scope may
include:

- context-aware assistance and recommendation boundaries;
- capability discovery, selection, composition, and quality evaluation;
- governed personalization and improvement feedback;
- provenance, explainability, confidence, and human-control signals; and
- measurement of benefit, error, drift, and unintended impact.

Its design must inherit v0.2 safety controls and may not expand its own authority.
Detailed behavior is subject to the v0.3 architecture and governance process.

## v0.1 non-implementation boundary

v0.1 initializes this scope with documentation only. It implements no model,
memory, recommender, optimizer, learning loop, tool integration, runtime service,
UI/UX, dependency, or v0.3 behavior. No enhancement capability is available or
promised by this directory.
